How do I play?

Each field hides exactly one asteroid in every row, column, and colored region, and asteroids never touch — not even at the corners.

  • Tap a square to mark it empty (X).
  • Double-tap to place a targeting reticle where you think an asteroid is.
  • Drag across squares to mark or clear a whole streak at once.
  • When all targets are placed, open the red safety cover and press LAUNCH.

What are Echo Crystals?

Echo Crystals power the radar. Pull the radar lever to fire a scan that reveals one hidden asteroid. You earn points by clearing asteroids (1 point each) and spend 10 points per crystal in the shop. New players get 5 free crystals from the field manual. Crystals are earned through gameplay only — there is nothing to buy with real money.

What are field seeds?

Every field has a short seed code (tap it at the top of the screen). Share the code and anyone can warp to your exact field. Seeds are 1–6 characters and case-sensitive.

The sound stopped / isn't working

  • Check the Music and Sound Effects sliders in Settings (zero = muted).
  • The game respects your silent switch and won't interrupt other apps' audio.
  • If sound stopped after switching apps, force-quitting and reopening the game resets audio.

I want to start over

Settings → Reset Game (at the bottom). This erases all progress, points, crystals, stats, and unlocks, and cannot be undone.

How do I unlock bigger grids?

Clear 10 distinct 6×6 fields to unlock 8×8, then 10 distinct 8×8 fields to unlock 10×10. Progress is shown on the locked buttons.
