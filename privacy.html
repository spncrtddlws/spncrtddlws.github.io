🔒 Privacy Policy — Generic Space Fun Time Game

Effective date: __LAUNCH_DATE__

The short version

This game collects nothing about you. There is no account, no login, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Everything the game needs to remember (your progress, settings, pilot name) stays on your device and never leaves it.

Data collection

We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app:

  • does not require an account or sign-in
  • does not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • does not serve ads or work with ad networks
  • does not track you across apps or websites
  • does not access your contacts, photos, location, microphone, or any other device data

On-device storage

Your game progress, settings, statistics, and pilot name are stored locally on your device using the operating system's standard storage. Deleting the app deletes this data. Using the in-game "Reset Game" option erases it as well.

Children

Because no data is collected from anyone, there is nothing special to say about children — the same zero-collection policy applies to all players.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
